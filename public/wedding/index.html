GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

Does the wedding website include online RSVP?+

Yes. The basic public invitation includes the event name, date, location and online RSVP. Guests respond in their browser and you follow responses in the app.

Can we personalise our wedding invitation?+

Yes, with Premium. Photos, styles, colours, fonts and messages help your invitation reflect the celebration. The free public invitation uses the standard design.

Can we organise wedding seating?+

Yes. Seating lives alongside your guest list in the app. Assign guests to tables and share table lookup by link or QR code according to your event access.
