How a Planne invitation works

The organizer uses Planne on Android to prepare the event. Guests open the invitation in a browser, see the details, and RSVP without installing the app. Their responses belong with the plan, so your next decision starts with a clearer guest count.

  1. 01

    Plan the essentials

  2. 02

    Share one invitation link

    Send it in the channel you choose. Guests can open it on their phone or computer.

  3. 03

    Follow the replies

    RSVP responses help you plan food, seating, and the practical details.

FAQ

Before you share the link

Do guests need the Planne app?

No. Guests open the invitation and RSVP in a browser. The organizer plans the event in the Android app.

Can I create an invitation for free?

Yes. Planne includes a basic public invitation with RSVP. Premium features and current prices are available in the app.

Can I change the invitation design?

Premium lets you customize photos, colors, fonts, and messages. You can preview the available styles before creating your own.

Does Planne send the invitation for me?

You share the invitation link using the messaging or communication channel you choose.
