GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

Is Planne a website or an app?+

You organize your event in the Android app and share the event website with your guests. They read the details and RSVP in their browser without installing Planne.

Can I plan more than weddings?+

Yes. Planne supports birthdays, baby showers, quinceañeras, graduations and other celebrations. Each event has its own guests, budget, tasks and vendors.

What works offline?+

Local planning is saved on your device. Publishing invitations, receiving responses and uploading photos need an internet connection. Content sync for your own events is part of Premium.
