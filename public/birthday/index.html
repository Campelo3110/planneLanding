GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

Can I use Planne for a kids’ birthday party?+

Yes. Create the event in your app and share the invitation with parents or guardians. The guest list supports guests, plus-ones and children.

Do my guests need an app?+

No. Guests open your birthday link in their browser to read the details and RSVP. The planning stays in your Planne app.

Does it work for adult birthday parties?+

Yes. The same planning tools support dinners, house parties and larger celebrations. Choose the features that make sense for your occasion.
